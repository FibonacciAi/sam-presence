How Sam
stays present.

A fast, bounded loop: perception stays local, while Jev adds typed context alongside the voice conversation.

01 / LOCAL

See the moment.

Browser camera input becomes coarse movement, head, gaze, and gesture signals through MediaPipe. Raw frames never leave the device.

02 / EPHEMERAL

Compress gently.

The browser emits compact observations for the current turn — short-lived context, not a recording or a verdict.

03 / PARALLEL

Let Jev notice.

Jev receives structured text, then returns typed relevance, grounding, and perspective signals. It never receives raw camera.

04 / REALTIME

Keep talking.

Grok’s open-mic voice responds as you finish speaking, using the latest context. Jev enriches subsequent responses. Hold-to-talk can wait for Jev before replying.

Inspect the boundary

camera → MediaPipe → {movement, head, gaze, gesture} → ephemeral observations → Jev. Jev’s typed signals join the next voice turn. Audio-reactive color pulses follow actual playback, not inferred emotion.

Hear the thread.

Voice gives the moment shape, so conversation can stay fluid and responsive.

Notice, lightly.

Attention and expression are treated as bounded signals — never definitive emotions or mindreading.

Keep it clear.

Sam works from compact observations and structured context, with your permission.

Privacy, by design: camera frames stay on your device. Only voice and compact observations are sent to providers, and only with permission.
